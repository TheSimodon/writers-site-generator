A little about me

I am, born in 1996 on the Baltic Sea coast, and am a freelance actor and writer. From 2015 to 2019, I trained as an actor at the Theaterakademie Vorpommern; afterward I studied cultural studies, literature, and music at the University of Hildesheim. Today, I perform in the independent theater scene and write short stories and plays.
